Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Color Picker Pro is committed to your privacy. This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data Storage

All colors, history, and palettes are saved locally on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions Used

  • storage — Save colors and palettes locally on your browser.
  • clipboardWrite — Copy color values to your clipboard.
  • scripting + activeTab + tabs — Inject the floating widget on the current page only when you explicitly click the pin button.

No Analytics

Zero analytics, zero tracking, zero third-party scripts.
